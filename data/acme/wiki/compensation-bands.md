Engineering salary bands for India, effective April 2026.

Software Engineer I: 18 to 24 lakh per year. Software Engineer II: 26 to 36 lakh per year. Senior Software Engineer: 40 to 58 lakh per year.

Bands are reviewed every April. Offers above the top of a band need approval from the CFO.
