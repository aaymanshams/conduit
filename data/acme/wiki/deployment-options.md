title: Deployment Options
groups: everyone
updated_at: 2026-06-10T00:00:00Z
---
The platform can run as multi-tenant SaaS, in a single-tenant private cloud, or on-premises inside the customer's own network.

On-premises deployments need a Kubernetes cluster with at least 3 worker nodes, 48 vCPUs and 192 GB of memory in total. Customer data never leaves their network; model calls go to a model endpoint the customer chooses.

Data residency is supported in India, the UAE, the EU and the US. Moving an existing customer between regions takes about two weeks and is handled by the forward deployed engineering team.
