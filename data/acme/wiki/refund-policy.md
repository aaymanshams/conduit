---
title: Refund and Credit Policy
groups: everyone
updated_at: 2026-08-01T00:00:00Z
---
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. We do not refund the current month, but no further charges are made.

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of the start of the contract. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but unused months can be converted into service credits that are valid for 12 months.

Service credits for SLA breaches are calculated under the Support SLA policy and are applied to the next invoice automatically. Credits are never paid out as cash.

Any exception to this policy needs written approval from the VP Customer Success.
