groups: everyone
updated_at: 2026-07-15T00:00:00Z

Every ticket gets a priority from P1 to P4 when it is created.

P1 means production is down or all users are blocked. First response within 30 minutes, 24x7, and a status update every 2 hours until resolved. A P1 open for more than 8 hours is escalated to the VP Customer Success.

P2 means a major feature is degraded with no workaround. First response within 4 business hours.

P3 is a question or a minor issue with a workaround. First response within 1 business day. P4 is feedback or a feature request, answered within 5 business days.

If we miss a P1 or P2 first-response target, the customer receives a service credit of 5 percent of that month's fee, capped at 25 percent per month.
